Privacy

Built so nobody feels watched.

They say yes first

Nobody is paired until the person sharing their location approves it.

No live tracking

Their location is read only when you ask. There's no map that follows them around.

They always know

Every time their location is shared, their phone shows it.

Stop in one tap

Either of you can unpair at any time. Auto-answer and alerts switch off in one tap.

Low-battery alert

If their phone dies, you won't be left guessing.

When their battery reaches the level they pick, their phone sends you its last location once, before it goes quiet.

  • Off until they turn it on, and they pick the level: 5 to 20%.
  • One alert per charge, not a stream of updates.
  • Checked about every 15 minutes, so it may arrive a few percent late.

Questions

Good to know.

Is PingSafe free?

Yes. No account, no ads, nothing to buy.

Why isn't it on the Play Store?

PingSafe is in testing. Until it's on the Play Store you install the file from this page, and the app tells you when a new version is ready.

Does it drain the battery?

Very little. PingSafe never tracks location in the background. Their phone sleeps until you ask, reads its location once, and sleeps again. The only background task is the low-battery alert, and only if they turn it on: it checks the battery level (not the location) about every 15 minutes.

Can someone see my location without me knowing?

No. You approve every pairing, your phone shows every time your location is shared, and you can unpair in one tap.

What does the other person see?

The place, how precise it is, your battery, whether you're charging, your network and the time. Our server keeps only the latest answer, for about an hour.

Does it work on iPhone?

Not yet. PingSafe is for Android 8 or newer, on both phones.

Is the file safe to install?

It comes straight from PingSafe's GitHub page and is signed with our certificate (the SHA-256 is listed above). Android only installs updates signed with the same certificate.
